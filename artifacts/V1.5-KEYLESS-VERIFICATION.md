# TOURPLUS V1.5 keyless verification

Verified locally on 2026-10-23. **Implementation and offline verification complete; real Meta accounts are not connected or certified.**

## Delivered behavior

WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger and Instagram direct messages enter separate signed webhook endpoints. Account-scoped events create CRM leads and conversation history once, even on duplicate delivery. Authorized staff can reply, approve AI drafts or create a new enquiry after a closed trip. Pending AI work respects human takeover and current lead ownership.

Durable database queues isolate receiving from generation and sending. Delivery/read callbacks converge without regressing. An uncertain provider outcome blocks further automatic dispatch until evidence reconciles it; socket failures and server errors never trigger blind resends. Bounded recovery retains the lease for attempts beyond its current scan, and consistent advisory lock ordering prevents nested channel/staff row-lock inversions.

Every new exposed table has RLS and explicit grants. Staff read through their existing lead ownership policies; queues, receipts, events and worker lease credentials stay private. Provider recipients come from stored conversations rather than caller-supplied identifiers. Credentials remain server-only. Channels default to disabled and require explicit account configuration, send enablement and pilot recipients.

## Limits and next gate

Offline tests cannot prove Meta token permissions, app review, subscription routing, approved templates, actual device delivery or model quality. They also cannot replace native PostgreSQL multi-session load/deadlock testing; PGlite runs a serialized connection. The AI generation service was substituted, so real-model output remains a staging check.

Next, use **Meta test assets and staging-only credentials** with a separate staging database and HTTPS deployment. Follow the acceptance matrix in [the setup guide](../docs/channel-setup.md), including real login/role changes, per-channel receive/reply/receipt checks, a hosted scheduler, recovery and multilingual AI review. Production keys can remain absent throughout that stage. After staging passes, production activation is a separate step.

Media capture currently stores safe metadata and asks staff to review the original provider inbox. Media download/transcription, sending attachments, lead-ad forms, comments, historic inbox import and cross-channel identity merging are outside this initial direct-chat release.

The implementation is saved locally for review. It has not been pushed to the automatically deploying production branch.
